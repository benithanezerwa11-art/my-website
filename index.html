Why Rear Ducks?

Duck rearing can provide food, income and an opportunity for farmers to develop a small livestock project.

Ducks need proper housing, good food, clean water and regular care to grow well and remain healthy.

🏠 Housing

Provide ducks with a clean, dry and safe shelter that protects them from bad weather and predators.

💧 Clean Water

Ducks need clean drinking water every day. Keep their water containers clean.

🌾 Good Food

Give ducks suitable feed and enough nutrients for healthy growth.

🛡️ Protection

Protect ducks from predators by keeping their housing secure, especially at night.

🇷🇼 Duck Rearing in Rwanda

Duck farming can be a useful activity for farmers and families in Rwanda. Ducks can be raised for eggs, meat, breeding and income generation.

🌱 What Ducks Need

  • Safe and clean housing
  • Clean drinking water
  • Suitable and nutritious feed
  • Protection from predators
  • Regular cleaning and observation

📍 Finding Help in Rwanda

Duck farmers in Rwanda can seek advice from veterinary and agricultural professionals when they need help with animal health, feeding, diseases, treatment or general duck care.

    🐣 Finding Duck Breeds in Rwanda

    Farmers looking for duck breeds can contact local livestock officers, veterinary professionals and reputable poultry breeders to ask about available breeds.

    • Ask local livestock and agricultural officers.
    • Contact qualified veterinary professionals.
    • Look for reputable poultry and livestock breeders.
    • Choose breeds that are suitable for your farming goals.
  • Veterinary services: Contact a qualified veterinary professional for advice about duck health, diseases and treatment.
  • District agriculture and livestock services: Local agricultural officers can provide information about livestock farming and available support.
  • RAB: The Rwanda Agriculture and Animal Resources Development Board provides information and support related to agriculture and livestock.

  • District agriculture and livestock offices – can help farmers find local livestock services and advice.
