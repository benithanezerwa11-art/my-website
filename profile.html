How To Rear Ducks?

Duck rearing involves providing ducks with suitable housing, food, clean water, and proper care.

Things Ducks Need

  • A safe and clean place to live
  • Clean drinking water
  • Suitable food
  • Protection from predators
  • Regular cleaning
