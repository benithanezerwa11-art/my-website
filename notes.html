How to Rear Ducks

Home About Duck Care

Duck Housing

Provide ducks with a safe, clean and comfortable place to live. The housing should protect them from bad weather and predators.

Feeding Ducks

Ducks need suitable food and regular access to clean drinking water.

Clean Water

Make sure ducks have clean water available every day. Keep their water containers clean.

Keeping Ducks Clean

Clean the duck housing regularly and remove dirty bedding to help maintain a healthy environment.

Daily Care

  1. Check the ducks each day.
  2. Provide food.
  3. Provide clean drinking water.
  4. Keep their housing clean.
  5. Watch for signs that a duck may be unwell.
